How to connect your Door Device?

To connect your biometric device (ZKTECO, Hikvision, etc.) to the system:

  1. Ensure the device is on the same local network (LAN) as the server, or has a Static Public IP.
  2. The default port for ZKTeco devices is 4370.
  3. Enable TCP/IP communication in the device network settings.
  4. Enter the Serial Number exactly as printed on the device label.

Push Protocol (Real-time Integration):

For real-time door opening and attendance, use the Push SDK API. Enter the following redirect URL in your device's "ADMS" or "Web Server" settings:

https://your-domain.com/biometric/api/push/
